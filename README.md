# 🎬 *Fifty Shades of Grey*

## 📖 Overview
*Fifty Shades of Grey* is a 2015 romantic drama film directed by **Sam Taylor-Johnson** and based on the novel by *E. L. James*.  
It explores the relationship between **Anastasia Steele**, a college graduate, and **Christian Grey**, a wealthy entrepreneur with a dark secret.

---

## 🧩 Main Characters
- **Anastasia Steele** – A shy and curious college student.
- **Christian Grey** – A mysterious billionaire with a complicated past.
- **Kate Kavanagh** – Anastasia’s best friend.
- **José Rodriguez** – Anastasia’s close friend and aspiring photographer.
